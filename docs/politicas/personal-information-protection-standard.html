1. Purpose and scope

This standard sets the mandatory rules for how Pólia One collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes personal information. It covers personal information of Pólia One users (sellers) and any personal information received from sales channels connected by those users, including TikTok Shop. It applies to all Pólia systems, to the people who operate them and to every sub-processor. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and the TikTok Shop Partner data requirements.

2. Roles and accountability

  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): Silvia Nunes de Oliveira, privacidade@usepolia.com.br. Owns this standard, approves any new use of personal information, handles data subject and partner requests, and leads breach response.
  • Controller and processor: Pólia is the controller of sellers' account data. For data received from TikTok Shop on a seller's behalf, Pólia acts as a processor and uses it only to deliver the service the seller enabled.
  • Anyone with access signs a confidentiality agreement before first access, follows this standard and reports any suspected incident to the DPO immediately.

3. Principles

  1. Purpose limitation: personal information is used only for the purpose stated in section 4.
  2. Minimization: only the fields needed for that purpose are stored; everything else is discarded.
  3. Transparency: the public privacy policy (usepolia.com.br/privacidade) describes every use.
  4. Security: encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, no secrets in code (section 6).
  5. Limited retention: data is deleted when its purpose ends (section 7).
  6. Accountability: every rule here is enforced in code or in a documented process, and checked (Appendix A).

Buyer personal information is not stored. Order payloads are processed in memory; the buyer's name, address, phone number, email and any other buyer identifier are discarded and never written to the database, to logs or to backups. Pólia does not use TikTok Shop data for advertising, profiling, model training or contacting buyers, does not sell or share it, and does not combine it with data from other sources about the same buyer.

5. Seller (user) personal information

  • Collected: name, email, login data, business information the seller types in, subscription and payment status (card data is handled only by Stripe and never reaches Pólia).
  • Legal basis (LGPD art. 7): performance of the contract with the seller; legal obligation for tax records; consent for optional marketing email, which can be withdrawn at any time.

6. Security controls applied to personal information

  • Encryption: TLS on every connection; database encrypted at rest (AES-256, Supabase managed PostgreSQL); TikTok Shop access and refresh tokens stored in Supabase Vault, encrypted and readable only by server-side code.
  • Isolation: row-level security is enabled on every table. Tables holding TikTok Shop connection, events, listing links and sync queues allow no access from the browser at all: only server-side processes can read them.
  • Least privilege: production access limited to the DPO, with multi-factor authentication on every administrative account; no shared accounts.
  • Integrity of incoming data: TikTok Shop webhooks are accepted only with a valid HMAC signature; a shop is linked to an account only after the seller confirms it on screen.
  • Logs: errors are logged without personal information or tokens.
  • Development: every release passes a security checklist; database security advisors are checked after each schema change; a dedicated security review of the TikTok Shop integration was completed on October 24, 2026, before release.

8. Data subject and partner requests

  • Rights under LGPD art. 18 (confirmation, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent) are handled through privacidade@usepolia.com.br within 15 days.
  • Requests from TikTok Shop or from a seller to delete, update or provide data received from TikTok Shop are handled through the same channel, with the same deadline. The seller can also disconnect the shop in Pólia One or revoke authorization in TikTok Shop Seller Center at any time.
  • The requester's identity is verified before data is provided or deleted.

9. Sharing, sub-processors and international transfer

  • Sub-processors: Supabase (database, United States, us-east-2), Cloudflare (application hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional email), Google (Analytics, Calendar and Gemini, for seller-provided data only). TikTok Shop data is processed only by Supabase and Cloudflare.
  • Each sub-processor is bound by a data processing agreement. A new sub-processor requires DPO approval and an update to the privacy policy before it receives any data.
  • Transfer to the United States relies on the safeguards of LGPD art. 33 (contractual clauses in the providers' data processing agreements).

10. Breach notification

  1. Contain: revoke affected tokens and keys, block access, fix the cause.
  2. Assess: which data, whose, how much and for how long.
  3. Notify affected sellers and TikTok Shop by email within 48 hours of a suspected or confirmed breach involving TikTok Shop data; notify data subjects and the ANPD when there is relevant risk or harm (LGPD art. 48).
  4. Record the incident, root cause and corrective actions.

11. Awareness and review

Anyone granted access reads this standard and the security policy before first access and again after every update. The standard is reviewed at least once a year, and earlier when the law changes, a new data type or sub-processor is added, or an incident occurs.
